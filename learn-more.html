About Lynch Lion Pride

The Lynch Lion Pride is the parent-teacher organization for M.A. Lynch. Our goal is to help families connect, support teachers and staff, and build a stronger school community for our students.

  • MeetingsEvery 2nd Monday of the month at 4:30 pm in the school library.
  • What we doFamily events, fundraisers, staff appreciation, teacher support, and volunteer opportunities.
  • Get involvedFill out the interest form and connect with the PTO to learn how you'd like to help.

Our Mission

We want to create opportunities for parents to connect, invest in their child’s school, and build community between parents and teachers.

“Our mission is to create opportunities for parents to connect with each other, invest in their child’s school with their time, talents, resources, and build community between parents and teachers to better help meet the needs of our school.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are a few quick answers about the PTO.

What is the LLP? Also known as the PTO / PTA / PTC?

This is the Parent-Teacher Organization, formally called the Lynch Lion Pride.

When do we meet?

Every 2nd Monday of the month at 4:30 pm in the school library.

What is the goal of the Lynch Lion Pride?

Our goal is to create opportunities for parents to connect, invest in their child’s school, and build community between parents and teachers to better help meet the needs of our school.

What does the Lynch Lion Pride do?

We carry out volunteer opportunities to relieve teacher and staff workloads, plan school-wide events to connect families, and run fundraisers that give back to teachers, staff, and students at M.A. Lynch.

How do I become a volunteer?

Sign up on the school district website and get cleared to be on campus.

How do I get updates on events?

Follow the PTO on Facebook and Instagram. Events are also published in the school’s monthly newsletter, the ROAR.
